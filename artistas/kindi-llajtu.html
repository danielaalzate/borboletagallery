Kindi Llajtu

An Inga painter whose practice transforms colour, line and ancestral memory into spaces for contemplation.

About the artist

Colour as a
living territory.

Kindi Llajtu Álvarez Jacanamijoy is a Colombian painter from the Inga people, born in Manoy, Santiago, Putumayo. His visual language draws on the memory, colours and ancestral knowledge he encountered as a child with his grandparents.

After studying Fine Arts at the National University of Colombia, he presented his work in solo and group exhibitions in Colombia, Mexico and France. In his paintings, a line can become a river, a tree, a horizon or a vessel: a living form that contracts and expands.

Through layers of acrylic and oil paint, he creates chromatically intense surfaces with a tactile appearance. His work connects contemporary experience with Inga wisdom, proposing colour as a space of memory, movement and relation to territory.
